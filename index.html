About CampusCash

CampusCash is a small money manager made for students. Add what comes in from a job or home, log what you spend, and see what’s left, month by month.

Everything is saved only in this browser, on this device. There are no accounts, no ads, and nothing is uploaded. This is not a cloud backup: clearing site data or switching devices removes your entries.

Budgets are optional. Use one if a limit helps you, and ignore it if it doesn’t.

Your privacy comes first. We don’t collect your data: no sign-up, no analytics, no trackers, no cookies, no ads. Your entries, budget and card wallet live only in this browser’s storage and are never sent to us or anyone else. The only thing that ever leaves your device is a suggestion you choose to send by email. “Hide” on the card wallet only covers the numbers on screen. For real protection, turn on the PIN lock, which encrypts the wallet on this device. A strict security policy also stops this page from connecting anywhere else.
